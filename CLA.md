# Merit Order Individual Contributor License Agreement

Version 1

## What this is, in short

This summary explains the agreement below. It isn't part of the agreement; if the two differ, the agreement wins.

- **What signing means.** You give Espeto LLC, which maintains Merit Order, a license to use what you contribute,
  and a license to any of your patents that your contribution needs. You also confirm that the work is yours to give.
- **You keep your copyright.** This is a license, not a transfer of ownership. You can still use your own work
  however you like, including in other projects.
- **Why the project asks.** Merit Order is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0-only. Espeto LLC wants to be able to offer
  it under other licenses as well, for example a commercial license to a company that can't use AGPL software. It
  can only do that for code it has the right to license that way, so it asks each contributor for that right. The
  AGPL version stays available either way.
- **How to sign.** Open a pull request. A bot will comment and ask you to sign. Reply on the pull request with this
  exact comment:

  > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

  The bot records your GitHub account, the pull request and the time, and your signature then covers all your future
  contributions too. You sign once per version of this agreement.
- **Contributing for a company?** If your employer has rights in what you write (many employment contracts give it
  that), your employer needs to agree as well. Contact the maintainer through the contact details on their GitHub
  profile ([github.com/therick](https://github.com/therick)) to arrange a Corporate Contributor License Agreement
  before you sign this one.
- **New versions.** If this agreement changes, its version number goes up, and contributors may be asked to sign the
  new version for later contributions. Contributions already made stay under the version you signed.
